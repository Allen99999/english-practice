# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Taiwanese students from junior high (翰林版 grades 7–9) through GEPT Elementary and TOEFL iBT preparation, plus their teacher Allen. Students mostly practice on iPads and phones, often in short sessions after class or at home; Allen reviews results, grades writing/speaking and tracks progress on a laptop. The job: practice exam-style English questions, get immediate feedback, review mistakes and vocabulary, and see progress over time.

## Product Purpose
Apex English is a self-paced English practice platform built and run by one teacher. It turns textbook and exam content (school tests, GEPT, TOEFL 2026) into adaptive practice, mock exams, AI-graded writing and a personal vocabulary list, while syncing every attempt to the teacher. Success means students come back on their own, practice more, and know exactly which skill (listening, speaking, reading, writing) to work on next.

## Brand Personality
Premium, calm, encouraging. The interface should feel like a well-made Apple app (Liquid Glass, restrained monochrome, generous spacing), with small moments of game-like delight (combo, levels, a personal player card) that reward effort without distracting from the questions. Voice in Traditional Chinese: friendly, direct, never childish.

## Anti-references
- Over-animated UIs: Allen explicitly rejected a version where every tap made the screen flash; motion must feel natural and quick.
- Busy, multi-color "edtech for kids" looks with cartoon clutter on every screen.
- Generic SaaS dashboards (hero metric + gradient accent + identical card grids).
- Anything that slows down answering a question or competes with the question for attention.

## Design Principles
1. Questions first: during a quiz nothing decorative moves or competes with the content.
2. Delight at the edges: celebrations, opening animation and the player card live at entry, exit and milestones, and are always skippable.
3. Calm premium: one restrained palette, glass used with purpose, motion that eases out and settles.
4. Progress you can read at a glance: every record turns into a clear next step (which skill to practice, which error repeats).
5. Teacher in the loop: anything a student sees (scores, AI feedback) is visible and correctable by the teacher.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast in both light and dark themes. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for every animation (crossfade or instant). Touch targets sized for iPad/phone use; all decorative animations skippable by tap. Text in Traditional Chinese with English content; no information conveyed by color alone (scores also shown as numbers/labels).
